What changes because of your boards

  • 7/16″ OSB instead of 12 mm (1/32″ thinner). Outside sizes and every hole position stay as designed; pieces that fit between others come out up to 1/16″ longer. All sizes in this plan already include that.
  • 5/8″ floor, no shim. The sandbox floor top sits 5/32″ higher than the design. The pedestal walls and sandbox walls are 5/32″ shorter to match, so the pedestal top, the turret and both conduit fittings stay at their design heights and still line up with the control box. Hole heights in those walls are already corrected.
  • Elbow support (printed P16): it stands on the sandbox floor, so it is now 5/32″ too tall. Saw off its 5/32″ base plate flush with the post (drawing on the right) and fix the post with one #6 × 1-1/4″ screw up through the floor into its centre (1/8″ pilot) plus a few drops of CA or epoxy. Nothing else printed is affected.
  • Spacer stack is now 5 × 7/16″: the chamber between the fittings is 3 1/8″ long — the PTFE tubes’ bow needs about 11/16″ radius, still above the 5/8″ minimum Derived. The arm’s slew axis moves 2 3/8″ toward the box.

Ropes and tubes are shorter than the manual by the 2 7/16″ the conduit run lost (arm ropes twice). Cut ropes 3/4″ long and trim when you crimp.

Working with these materials Assumption

Particle board and sand. Particle board swells and crumbles if it gets damp. Seal both faces and all four edges of the floor (paint, poly or a coat of wood glue thinned with water) before assembly, and keep the sand dry.
OSB edges. Screws hold less well in OSB edges than in plywood. Drill a 3/32″ pilot for every #6 screw, keep screws 1/2″ from corners, and glue every joint.
