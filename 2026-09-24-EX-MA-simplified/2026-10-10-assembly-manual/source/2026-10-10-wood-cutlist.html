What to buy

  • One 4 × 8 ft sheet of 1/2″ plywood (sanded 15/32″ or 12 mm Baltic birch) covers either version; ask the store to make the first rip (R1) if the sheet will not fit your car.
  • Project panels work too: the sandbox floor is exactly a 2 × 2 ft panel (24″ — the CAD size is 1/64″ more, irrelevant); the rest of either version fits on two 2 × 4 ft panels plus the offcut of a third.
  • Sizes assume 12 mm (15/32″) stock. With true 1/2″ the boxes come out up to 1/16″ bigger outside — no change needed.

Before you cut

  • All sizes in inches, to the nearest 1/16″. The small grey mm figures are the exact CAD sizes, for metric tapes.
  • Layouts allow a 1/8″ saw kerf between pieces. Holes are given as standard drill-bit sizes.
  • IDs W01–W21 are the same as in the assembly manual; W22/W23 exist only in version B.
  • Write the ID on every piece in pencil as soon as it is cut, and mark its bottom edge — hole positions are measured from it.
  • Drill the box front (W02) and the sandbox wall −u (W18) clamped together so their holes line up.

Version B in one paragraph

Control box 8-7/16″ × 14-1/4″ × 6-1/8″ (the 2026-09-28 compact spec). Pedestal 1/2″ shorter on its +u side and pushed 2-1/4″ toward the box; a two-layer plywood spacer (W22, W23) closes the gap to the sandbox wall instead of the PVC conduit. Page 6 explains it.

Minimal version: what changes

SectionVersion B wall stack on the conduit axis, to scale; each plywood layer 15/32″: W02 box front, W18 sandbox wall, W22 + W23 spacers, W10 pedestal wall. One 8-32 rod clamps everything. The spacers' 3-15/16″ square window holds the rod nuts' far ends clear and gives the PTFE tubes room to bow.

Why a spacer, not nothing Derived

The four arm PTFE tubes change length by up to about 7/16″ as the boom and stick move; in version A that spare length coils in the conduit. With the pedestal flat on the sandbox wall there would be only 1-7/8″ between the two fittings, and a tube storing that much there bends to about 5/16″ radius — below the 5/8″ minimum. With the 15/16″ spacer the chamber is 3-5/16″ long and the bow needs about a 3/4″ radius. The chamber is closed on all sides, so no sand gets in.

Estimate from a cosine bow: rise h = √(4·L·ΔL)/π, smallest radius = L² / (2π²·h). Not a CAD check — do the dry fit below.

Build notes

  • Glue W22 to W23, then glue the pair to the outside of the pedestal wall −u (W10), windows lined up with its Ø62 hole.
  • Stand the pedestal against the sandbox wall −u, run a bead of silicone round the spacer’s outer edge, and clamp with the four rods.
  • The arm’s slew axis now sits 2-1/4″ closer to the box: 5-1/4″ from the sandbox wall’s inside face instead of 7-1/2″.
  • Dry fit: before closing the box lid, move the boom and stick through their full ranges and check that each stick and bucket tube still slides freely at the turret end.
